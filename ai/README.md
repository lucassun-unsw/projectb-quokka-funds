# AI workflow pack (z5594806, Part B)

My record of how I used and checked AI on Part B, for the AI Workflow &
Transparency criterion.

- `AI_NOTES.md` — the running account, in my own words: how I direct and check
  the assistant, and a log of what it got wrong and how I caught it, with
  pointers into the per-task logs.
- `prompt_log_01` … `prompt_log_13` — one per task: what I wanted, what I
  directed, what the assistant produced, what was wrong or risky, and what I
  changed and why.

My agent instruction files are at the project root — `CLAUDE.md` and a short
`AGENTS.md` pointer — written by me for this project, not the provided stubs.

The interpretation and analysis prose in the report is my own work; the
assistant drafted code and report structure and checked my numbers, as the logs
describe.
